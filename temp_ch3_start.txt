338: **3.2 Project Planning**
339: 
340: The Toothwise project was planned around the screening and record-keeping workflow of CodiSmile Dental Clinic. The following considerations guided the planning of the system:
341: 
342: •  Project Objectives — to provide CodiSmile Dental Clinic with an AI-assisted dental screening system that detects possible cavities and related findings from an intraoral image, estimates a risk level, and generates a recommendation/prevention plan, while also supporting day-to-day clinic record-k...
343: 
344: •  Project Scope — the system covers staff authentication, patient management, dental screening and AI detection, risk assessment, recommendation generation, clinical notes, PDF reporting, audit logging, and a self-service patient portal.
345: 
346: •  Target Users — dentists and administrators at CodiSmile Dental Clinic, and the clinic's patients through the patient portal.
347: 
348: •  Clinic Workflow — a patient is registered or already on file, an intraoral image is captured or uploaded during a screening, the system produces detection, risk, and recommendation results for the dentist to review, and the dentist may add clinical notes or generate a PDF report.
349: 
350: •  Functional Requirements — detailed in Section 3.4.
351: 
352: •  Non-Functional Requirements — detailed in Section 3.4, covering security, usability, reliability, performance, and data protection.
353: 
354: •  AI Screening Requirements — the system was planned to use two independent detection models (YOLOv8 and YOLOv12) whose outputs are fused into a single clinical detection set, rather than relying on a single model.
355: 
356: •  Database Requirements — a relational PostgreSQL schema was planned to store dentists, patients, screenings, detections, recommendations, clinical notes, and audit logs with referential integrity between related records.
357: 
358: •  Security Requirements — JWT-based authentication, hashed passwords, rate-limited login endpoints, authenticated (non-public) image access, and audit logging were planned from the outset given the clinical and personal nature of the data involved.

389: 
390: **Table 3 Functional Requirements**
391: 
392: | ID | Requirement | Specification |
393: | :---- | :---- | :---- |
394: | **FR-01** | Staff authentication | Authenticate dentists and administrators by email and password and issue a JWT bearer token for authenticated API access. |
395: | **FR-02** | Role-based access | Distinguish administrator, dentist, and patient access scopes and restrict protected functions accordingly. |
396: | **FR-03** | Patient management | Create, view, update, and delete patient records and view screening history and risk trends. |
397: | **FR-04** | Dental screening | Submit an intraoral image in a single multipart screening request and receive detection, risk, recommendation, and screening-record results. |
398: | **FR-05** | AI detection | Run the configured YOLOv8 and YOLOv12 detectors, retain raw model outputs, and produce a fused clinical detection set. |
399: | **FR-06** | Risk assessment | Calculate a 0-100 risk score and classify the screening as LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH. |
400: | **FR-07** | Recommendation generation | Use the KNN recommender and condition-specific rules to generate an ordered prevention plan. |
401: | **FR-08** | Screening review | View screening images, fused detections, risk information, recommendations, clinical notes, and model-comparison information. |
402: | **FR-09** | PDF report | Generate an authenticated PDF screening report containing patient/screening information, findings, prevention plan, and clinical notes. |
403: | **FR-10** | Patient portal | Patients register, log in, and view their own screening history and screening details. |
404: | **FR-11** | Audit logging | Record relevant authentication and create/update/delete actions for administrative review. |
405: | **FR-12** | Health monitoring | Expose a health endpoint that checks database connectivity without exposing internal errors. |
406: | **FR-13** | Database backup | Provide a pg\_dump-based backup utility that creates timestamped, retained backups. |
