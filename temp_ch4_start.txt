763: | **first\_name** | VARCHAR | Staff member's first (given) name. |
764: | **email** | VARCHAR, UNIQUE | Login email; used for JWT authentication. |
765: | **hashed\_password** | VARCHAR | bcrypt password hash; plaintext is never stored. |
766: | **role** | ENUM (admin, dentist) | Determines access to admin-only functions (dentist management, audit log). |
767: | **license\_no** | VARCHAR, nullable | Dentist's professional license number. |
768: | **is\_active** | BOOLEAN | Enables disabling an account without deleting its history. |
769: | **created\_at** | TIMESTAMP | Record creation timestamp. |
