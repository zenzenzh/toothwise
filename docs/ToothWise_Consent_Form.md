### **2. Purpose and Nature of the Study**

The primary purpose of this study is to assess the quality, operational acceptability, performance, and clinical utility of **ToothWise**, an artificial intelligence-driven diagnostic support and clinical decision-recommendation system developed for CodiSmile Dental Clinic. ToothWise leverages the **YOLOv12 (You Only Look Once version 12)** deep learning architecture to perform real-time detection and localization of dental caries from clinical intraoral images, paired with an automated dental recommendation module.

The system evaluation is structured around the **ISO/IEC 25010 Software Product Quality Model**, a globally recognized standard for software engineering. The study seeks to gather quantitative and qualitative feedback from a target population of **one hundred (100) purposively selected respondents**, categorized into:
1. **IT Experts / Software Professionals:** Evaluating technical architecture, reliability, maintainability, portability, and security.
2. **Clinical End-Users (Dentists and Dental Staff):** Evaluating functional suitability, diagnostic efficacy, workflow integration, and usability.
3. **Patients / General End-Users:** Evaluating interface accessibility, clarity of recommendations, and overall user satisfaction.

---

### **3. Evaluation Criteria (ISO/IEC 25010 Quality Model)**

Your feedback will be gathered using a standardized survey instrument assessing eight (8) key software product quality characteristics:

1. **Functional Suitability:** Degree to which the system provides functions that meet stated cavity detection and treatment recommendation requirements.
2. **Performance Efficiency:** System responsiveness, inference speed of the YOLOv12 model, throughput, and resource utilization.
3. **Compatibility:** Ability of ToothWise to interoperate with existing clinic hardware, browser environments, and digital dental records.
4. **Usability:** Appropriateness, learnability, operability, user error protection, user interface aesthetics, and accessibility.
5. **Reliability:** System fault tolerance, diagnostic consistency, recovery capabilities, and operational availability.
6. **Security:** Protection of patient dental records, role-based authorization, confidentiality of diagnostic images, and data integrity.
7. **Maintainability:** Modularity of the system architecture, ease of updating detection models, and diagnostic log management.
8. **Portability:** Adaptability and ease of deployment across standard desktop, tablet, and workstation platforms.

---

### **4. Study Procedures**

If you agree to participate in this evaluation, your participation will proceed as follows:

1. **System Orientation & Demonstration (approx. 5–10 minutes):** A member of the research team will demonstrate the core features of the ToothWise system, including intraoral image acquisition/upload, YOLOv12 automated cavity detection and bounding box visualization, confidence scoring, diagnostic reporting, and treatment recommendations.
2. **Hands-On Interaction & System Testing (approx. 10–15 minutes):** You will be provided supervised, hands-on access to test the software using sample anonymized clinical dental imagery and simulate routine diagnostic workflows.
3. **Survey Questionnaire Administration (approx. 10 minutes):** Following system interaction, you will be requested to complete a structured evaluation survey questionnaire utilizing a 5-point Likert scale (5 - Strongly Agree to 1 - Strongly Disagree) along with open-ended feedback prompts.

The entire evaluation session will take approximately **25 to 35 minutes** of your time.

### **7. Confidentiality and Data Privacy Assurance (Republic Act No. 10173)**

The researchers adhere strictly to the principles of transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality mandated by the **Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)** and its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), as well as the research ethics standards of Universidad De Manila:

- **Anonymity & De-Identification:** Individual responses will remain confidential. Data gathered will be assigned alphanumeric identification codes (e.g., `EXP-01`, `DEN-03`, `PAT-12`) to prevent direct identification of any individual respondent.
- **Data Protection:** All survey questionnaires, digital response sheets, and evaluation metrics will be stored securely on encrypted, password-protected drives accessible exclusively to the four designated researchers and the capstone adviser.
- **Aggregate Reporting:** All findings presented in Chapter 4 (Results and Discussion), the capstone manuscript, journal publications, and oral defense presentations will be reported strictly in aggregate statistical form (means, standard deviations, percentage distributions, and thematic summaries).
- **Data Retention and Disposal:** In accordance with institutional research retention protocols, all collected raw survey forms and digital evaluation data will be permanently deleted and shredded after one (1) year following the final defense and submission of the approved capstone manuscript.
